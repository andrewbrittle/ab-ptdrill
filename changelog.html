What's new

The most recent changes will be listed first. Not every small fix makes the list — just the bits actually worth knowing about.

24 October 2026
  • New Frases tab, with the Sentence builder: one card from each coloured column - who, verb, what, where, with whom, when - and the sentence builds as you go, with the English underneath. Pick the verb and then its form: the person in Who and one easily mixed up with it, in the present, past and going-to.
  • Three modes in the builder and the challenge: Doing, Going (ir + where to + how - vou ao Porto de comboio) and Being: ser or estar, and the describing word made to agree - a comida está fria, os meus pais são simpáticos, a minha colega é advogada.
  • Reading challenge: a Portuguese sentence to read, picking the English for each part - including who and when from the verb form. Its part scores are kept separately.
  • Sentence challenge: an English sentence to build in Portuguese, checked part by part ("right verb - check the form"), one more go, then the answer. Scores are kept per part of the sentence, never touching your word greens.
  • Tabs in Portuguese, like the Japanese app: Palavras · Frases · Números · Gramática. Verbs moved into Palavras as a pill beside Basics / Travel / Life. Sound and Settings sit on the title line. The sentence card and the desk sheets moved to Frases.
  • A typed or said-out-loud answer that misses no longer lowers a word's score - a right one still raises it - so the hard modes never cost you your greens.
  • Sentences say "I" more often.
16 October 2026
  • Proper back navigation: inner pages have a "← …" link at the top left saying where it goes, and your phone's back gesture now goes one step back too (on a tab's main page it still does nothing, so you can't leave the app by accident). The ⚙ button closes Settings when tapped again. The Vocab tab no longer turns into "Back".
  • Fixed: word and verb cards on the selection screens show their confidence colour again.
  • One rule for picking words, everywhere: every list - topics, Groups and verbs - has Select all; lists of 21-30 also offer Random 10, bigger ones Random 20. The Drill button says what you'll get (Drill 20 words, Drill 20 forms from 54 verbs).
  • Group cards show how many words they hold, like the topic cards.
  • Verbs: tap a group heading (Irregular, -ar, -er, -ir) to select or clear the whole group; groups combine.
  • Verbs now has confidence bars: one for all verbs and one for each group (irregular, -ar, -er, -ir), for the tenses you've picked.
15 October 2026
  • Desk sheets (on the Grammar page): three printable pages of words to make up your own sentences from - pick one from each column. Doing something (who, how often, verb + what, where, with whom, when), Going somewhere (who, ir, where to, how, with whom, when) and Out and about (asking for things, food and drink, Onde fica…?, Quanto custa…?, and what you'll hear back). Shuffle for a new selection, then print or save as a PDF.
  • The Untested 20 card on the dice page now shows how many words you haven't been tested on yet, out of the total - e.g. (610 of 872).
14 October 2026
  • Sounds alike (on the dice page): hear a word and pick which of two it was - avó / avô, pão / pau, caro / carro, sono / sonho, falamos / falámos and 13 more pairs. After answering, tap either button to hear both. With sound off, read the English and pick the spelling.
  • Questions in the sentence card: a plan or event, asked about one part at a time - Quando é que o teu pai vai à farmácia? - Vai na segunda-feira. Who with, where, how, what, and yes/no questions answered the Portuguese way (Vai, sim.).
  • Answers in Portuguese (reversed drills, verbs, contractions, numbers) are now bold and full size instead of small and faded.
  • Search box: a × to clear it, and the search button sits on its own.
  • Settings shows how to install the app when it's open in a browser rather than installed.
12 October 2026
  • Quantities: 13 more, mostly the "not enough" side - não chega, demasiado pouco, poucos, demasiados, quase nada, quase nenhum, nem um, falta… - plus muitos, um bocadinho, mais ou menos and a maior parte.
  • The other half of the pairs: 49 words where a list had one side but not the other - feio, pesado / leve, barulhento, melhor / pior, vender, receber, responder, perder, esquecer-se, aquilo, ninguém, também não, anteontem, a horas, está mau tempo, a descer, que azar!, claro que não and more. With the quantities, they're the Latest batch in Latest words.
11 October 2026
  • Sentences rebuilt, on the Japanese app's model: who, when, where, with whom, how and what are mixed fresh each time, with checks so the mix makes sense (no museums at midnight, no flying to Sintra). Thousands of different sentences now: plans and what happened, getting up and going to bed (levanto-me, deitei-me), jobs, describing things (o quarto é pequeno, mas está limpo), the weather, likes and preferences, and two-line exchanges using tu. It's on the greeting card and at the top of the Grammar page.
  • The daily greeting now ends with a sentence - or a short exchange - built fresh each time: routines, plans, what happened yesterday, likes, ordering at a café, asking the way, opening times. Tap a line to hear it; tap ↻ for another.
  • Topics tidied: related lists merged (33 down to 21) and split across three pills - Basics, Travel and Life - instead of one long list. Scores are unaffected.
  • 250 more words in 11 new topics: position & compass, how you feel (tenho fome), everyday expressions (se calhar, pois, pronto), quantities, body & pharmacy, shops, phone & travel kit, countries & nationalities, time expressions, seasons & festivities, and jobs & free time. New Group: Sentence glue.
  • Numbers: random numbers and times to read, new every round. Times come both the everyday way (às duas e meia da tarde, três menos um quarto) and the timetable way (às catorze e trinta), with at / until / from / around. Plus look-ups for any number or time.
  • A daily greeting in Portuguese the first time you open the app each day - greeting, date and time, tap a line to hear it. Tap "Português" at the top to see it again.
  • Say it out loud: a new switch for vocab, verbs and contractions. See the prompt, say the answer, tap Show answer to see and hear it, then mark yourself. Speaking-speed recall, without spelling getting in the way.
  • Verbs: 51 everyday verbs in five tenses - present, past, was / used to, right now (estar a…) and going to (ir…). Pick verbs and tenses, then drill the forms; hold a verb to see its full table (tap a row to hear it).
  • Grammar: ten short rule cards (endings, past tenses, ser or estar, contractions, where me/te/se go, tu/você/o senhor, agreement), most with a drill attached.
  • A contractions drill - no, na, do, ao, pelo… filled into real sentences.
10 October 2026
  • First version: the Japanese drill app's engine with a European Portuguese starter vocabulary — 561 words in 22 topics, with Brazilian forms shown where they differ.
  • Typing is accent-aware: missing accents are accepted with a reminder (or marked wrong, if you prefer — see Settings).
